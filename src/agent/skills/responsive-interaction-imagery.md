# Responsive behavior, interaction, motion, and imagery

- Design responsive behavior as a change in composition, not a proportional shrink. Protect reading order, action visibility, tap targets, image crops, and content priority at representative desktop and mobile widths.
- Keep motion purposeful, brief, interruptible, and reduced-motion-safe. Avoid perpetual motion or hover effects that move the target.
- Use imagery with a defined art-direction job. Respect focal points, crop intentionally, preserve source quality, and add legibility protection without muddying the image.
- Reuse product-owned imagery and icon language. Avoid generic stock metaphors, decorative dashboards, synthetic blobs, random grids, and interchangeable AI-SaaS illustrations.
- Test interactive controls with keyboard and pointer input. Keep controls stable and their states obvious.
