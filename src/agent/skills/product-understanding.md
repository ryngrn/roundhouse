# Product understanding and scope

- Inspect the existing product before proposing or changing its interface. Read the requested page, nearby components, repository instructions, and configured design context.
- State the page's communication and product job in your working notes. Preserve established positioning unless the brief explicitly authorizes strategy changes.
- Keep changes inside the requested slice. Treat adjacent inconsistencies as observations, not permission to redesign them.
- Prefer an intentional evolution of the existing visual language. Reuse product assets, tokens, components, and interaction patterns before inventing replacements.
- If a missing decision would materially change positioning, scope, risk, spend, credentials, or an irreversible action, stop and identify that decision. Routine implementation choices are yours to make.
